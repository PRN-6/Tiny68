# User Guide — Tiny68

## 1. Welcome to Tiny68

Tiny68 is your desktop companion application. Small, animated creatures inhabit your screen, wander across application windows, climb on the taskbar, sleep, play, and interact with you.

---

## 2. Interaction Controls

- **Pick Up & Drag:** Click and hold your primary mouse button on any character to pick it up. Move the mouse to drag the character anywhere on your desktop.
- **Drop / Toss:** Release the mouse button. The character will fall under gravity and land safely on the taskbar or open windows!
- **Character Context Menu:** Right-click directly on any character or right-click the system tray icon to reveal management options.
- **Click-Through:** Clicking anywhere outside of characters passes through immediately to your regular desktop icons, web browser, code editor, or games.

---

## 3. System Tray Controls

Look for the Tiny68 icon in your Windows System Tray (near the clock):
- **Settings...:** Opens the settings configuration.
- **Add Character:** Spawn an additional Mochi or Kiko onto your desktop.
- **Pause / Resume Characters:** Freeze or resume simulation at any time.
- **Hide / Show Characters:** Temporarily hide characters when you need a distraction-free screen.
- **Performance Mode:** Choose Eco (battery saver), Balanced (default), Smooth (60 FPS), or Gaming mode.
- **Reset Positions:** Teleport all characters back onto the taskbar.
- **Exit Tiny68:** Terminate Tiny68 completely and clean up all resources.
